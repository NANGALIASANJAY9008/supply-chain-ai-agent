SUPPLIER DELIVERY POLICY

Document ID: DEL-001
Version: 1.0

1. DELIVERY EXPECTATIONS

Suppliers are expected to deliver products according to the agreed
purchase order schedule.

2. EXPECTED DELIVERY DATE

The expected delivery date is the date agreed for delivery of the order.

3. LATE DELIVERY

An order is considered late when the actual delivery occurs after the
expected delivery date.

4. DELIVERY DELAYS

Delivery delays should be monitored because they can affect:

- Production
- Customer fulfillment
- Inventory availability
- Operating costs
- Emergency procurement requirements

5. SUPPLIER PERFORMANCE

Repeated late deliveries should be included in supplier performance
evaluation.

6. CORRECTIVE ACTION

When delivery performance is consistently poor, the organization may
request corrective action from the supplier.

7. CRITICAL PRODUCTS

Critical products with high operational impact should receive additional
monitoring when delivery delays may create stock-out risk.
