SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT POLICY

Document ID: SUP-001
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines how suppliers are evaluated, monitored, and managed.

2. SUPPLIER PERFORMANCE

Supplier performance should be monitored using measurable indicators.

Important indicators include:

- Reliability score
- Average delivery delay
- Lead time
- Product quality
- Order fulfillment performance

3. SUPPLIER RELIABILITY

A supplier with a higher reliability score is generally preferred over
a supplier with a lower reliability score when other commercial factors
are comparable.

4. DELIVERY PERFORMANCE

Repeated delivery delays should trigger supplier performance review.

Suppliers with consistently high average delivery delays may require:

- Corrective action
- Performance review
- Renegotiation
- Alternative sourcing

5. SUPPLIER REVIEW

Supplier performance should be reviewed periodically.

Critical suppliers should receive additional monitoring when their
performance may affect business continuity.

6. SUPPLIER RISK

Supplier risk may increase when:

- Reliability decreases
- Delivery delays increase
- Lead times become unpredictable
- Product quality deteriorates
- A supplier becomes difficult to replace

7. ALTERNATIVE SUPPLIERS

For critical products, organizations should consider maintaining
alternative suppliers when commercially and operationally practical.
