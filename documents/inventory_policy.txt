INVENTORY MANAGEMENT POLICY

Document ID: INV-001
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines principles for maintaining adequate inventory while
controlling excess inventory.

2. CURRENT STOCK

Current stock represents the physical inventory available at a warehouse.

3. RESERVED STOCK

Reserved stock represents inventory that has been allocated to existing
requirements and should not normally be treated as freely available stock.

4. AVAILABLE STOCK

Available stock is calculated as:

Available Stock = Current Stock - Reserved Stock

5. REORDER LEVEL

The reorder level represents the inventory threshold at which replenishment
should be considered.

When available stock falls below the reorder level, the product should be
reviewed for replenishment.

6. LOW STOCK

A product is considered low stock when:

Available Stock < Reorder Level

Low-stock products should be prioritized according to operational
criticality and expected demand.

7. INCOMING INVENTORY

Incoming inventory represents stock expected from replenishment or
supplier orders.

Incoming stock should be considered when assessing future inventory
availability.

8. INVENTORY REVIEW

Inventory levels should be reviewed regularly for critical products.

Excess inventory should also be monitored because excessive stock can
increase storage and working-capital costs.
