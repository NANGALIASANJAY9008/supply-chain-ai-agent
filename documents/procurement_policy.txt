SUPPLY CHAIN PROCUREMENT POLICY

Document ID: PROC-001
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the standard procurement process for purchasing
products, raw materials, components, equipment, and other goods required
for supply chain operations.

2. PURCHASE REQUEST

All procurement activities must begin with a valid purchase request.

The purchase request should contain:

- Product or material identification
- Required quantity
- Required delivery date
- Requesting department
- Business justification
- Preferred supplier, if applicable

3. SUPPLIER SELECTION

Suppliers should be evaluated based on:

- Product quality
- Reliability
- Historical delivery performance
- Lead time
- Pricing
- Contract terms
- Compliance requirements

Supplier reliability and historical delivery performance should be
considered when selecting suppliers for critical products.

4. APPROVAL

Purchase requests must be reviewed before an order is placed.

High-value purchases may require additional management approval.

5. EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT

Emergency procurement may be used when a critical product or material
is required to prevent operational disruption.

Emergency purchases should be documented and reviewed after the purchase.

6. PURCHASE ORDER

A purchase order should include:

- Product identification
- Quantity
- Agreed price
- Supplier
- Expected delivery date
- Delivery location
- Applicable terms and conditions

7. RECORD KEEPING

Procurement records should be retained for audit and operational
purposes.

Relevant records include purchase requests, purchase orders, supplier
information, invoices, delivery records, and approval records.
